## 5. Emergency hotlines and flood alarms

**Marikina City Rescue 161** (run by CDRRMO), per [contact-us](https://www.marikina.gov.ph/contact-us):
- Landlines: **161** (also 8-161), 8-646-2436 to 38, 8-646-0427, 7-116-5532

- Radio Room / Tactical Operations Center: 646-1631 / 0927-968-4311
- National: **911** (national emergency line, not from the city site)

**Marikina River alarm levels** (Sto. Niño gauge). News reports from 2015-2025 cited below agree on these:

| Level | Water height | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1st alarm | 15.0 m | Alert: prepare; residents of low-lying areas should get ready to evacuate |
| 2nd alarm | 16.0 m | Prepare to evacuate / preemptive evacuation of riverside areas |
| 3rd alarm | 18.0 m | **Forced evacuation** |
| (17.0 m) | 17.0 m | The city has offered *voluntary/preemptive* evacuation at 17 m (Inquirer 2018) |

**Discrepancy:** Older GMA framing uses bands: Level 1 = 15-16 m, Level 2 = 16-17 m, Level 3 = above 17 m. More recent reports (Daily Tribune Jul 2025) put the third alarm and forced evacuation at 18 m. Confirm with CDRRMO before displaying. Sources: [Inquirer 2015](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/732341/marikina-river-rises-to-15-meter-warning-level-due-to-lando/amp), [Inquirer 2018](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1011620/marikina-river-still-at-second-alarm-as-water-rises-again), [GMA](https://gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/metro/661320/marikina-river-swells-to-17-meter-mark-2nd-alarm-still-in-place/story), [Tribune 2025](https://tribune.net.ph/2025/07/21/marikina-river-reaches-third-alarm).

**Evacuation centers:** I did not find an authoritative list. In practice the city uses public elementary and high schools and barangay covered courts; examples include Nangka ES, Malanday ES, H. Bautista ES, Concepcion Integrated School, Parang HS and Tumana covered courts. **Get the official list from CDRRMO.** School phone numbers are on the contact-us page.

## 6. Citizen services (general guidance; verify with the [Citizen's Charter](https://fdpp.marikina.gov.ph/citizen-charter))

These are national-standard requirements. Fees were **not verified** for Marikina. Online payments go through [payment.marikina.gov.ph](https://payment.marikina.gov.ph/), and forms are at [download-forms](https://www.marikina.gov.ph/download-forms).

- **Business permit (new or renewal):** Go to BPLO (online portal [bplo.marikina.gov.ph](https://bplo.marikina.gov.ph/client/index)). You need a DTI/SEC/CDA registration, a barangay business clearance, a lease contract or land title, a Community Tax Certificate (cedula), fire safety clearance (BFP) and sanitary permit (CHO). Renewals are due within January (Jan 1-20 is standard under the LGC).
- **Cedula (Community Tax Certificate):** At the City Treasury Office. Bring a valid ID. Individuals pay a ₱5 basic tax plus ₱1 per ₱1,000 of income (LGC Sec. 157).
- **Birth / marriage / death certificate:** For local copies, registration, late registration or corrections (RA 9048/10172), go to the Local Civil Registry (8646-0373). PSA-certified copies come from PSA Serbilis or a PSA outlet.
- **Real property tax:** Pay at the City Treasury and get assessments at the Assessor's Office. Bring the tax declaration or the previous official receipt. Paying in Q1 earns a discount (rate to be confirmed).
- **Building permit:** Through the Office of the Building Official (Engineering) or MCDA. Bring the title, tax declaration, signed and sealed plans, and a locational clearance (zoning).
- **Barangay clearance:** At your barangay hall. Bring proof of residency and an ID, plus a fee set by the barangay.
- **Senior citizen ID:** OSCA (8659-1956). For residents 60 and older, bring a birth certificate or valid ID showing age, proof of Marikina residency and 1x1 photos.
- **PWD ID:** Through the CSWDO or Persons with Disability Affairs Office. Bring a medical certificate stating the disability, a barangay certificate and photos (RA 7277 as amended).
- **Solo parent ID:** CSWDO. RA 11861 applies. Bring a barangay certificate of residency, proof of solo-parent category and an income statement.
- **Scholarships:** City scholarship program through the Mayor's Office / PLMar (Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Marikina). Details were not found.

## 7. Tourism and culture

- **Marikina Shoe Museum:** On J.P. Rizal St, Sta. Elena, beside the Kapitan Moy house. It displays Imelda Marcos's shoes. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikina_Shoe_Museum))
- **Kapitan Moy Residence / Sentrong Pangkultura ng Marikina:** Home of Laureano "Kapitan Moy" Guevarra, the father of Marikina shoemaking. The town council and NHCP declared it a national shrine in 1968. Phone 646-1823.
- **Our Lady of the Abandoned Parish (Nuestra Señora de los Desamparados):** Sta. Elena. The city fiesta is in May (verify the date; it is commonly cited as May 12).
- **Marikina River Park:** A riverbanks promenade (8541-7461), plus Marikina Sports Center and Marikina Cityhood Park.
- **Riverbanks Center:** A mall and outlet center on a former Marikina Textile Mills site in Barangka. The status of the current operator is unverified.
- **Giant shoes of Marikina:** Guinness-recognized giant shoes. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_shoes_of_Marikina))
- **Festivals:** Ka-Angkan Festival (honors clans and ancestors, held around the founding anniversary on April 16) and the Marikina Christmas Festival. The "Sapatos Festival" and shoe-industry events have been held around September and October; the dates are unverified.
- **Food:** Local dishes include *Everlasting* (an embutido-like dish cooked in a llanera), *Waknatoy* (menudo with pickles) and *Laoya* (a nilaga with garlic, kamote and saba) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikina#Culture)). The Gil Fernando Ave and Lilac St (SSS Village/Concepcion Dos) food strips are popular, but these are not from a cited source.
